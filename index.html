AI Governance Tools
Working prototypes that demonstrate AI governance at the intake, runtime, and fairness layers. Each uses deterministic rule engines mapped to real regulatory frameworks.
PROTOTYPE 01 LIVE
AI Use Case Intake & Risk Classifier
A structured intake tool for product teams submitting AI systems for governance review. Classifies risk level, maps to NIST AI RMF tiers and EU AI Act categories, and generates a governance assessment with recommended controls and an approval decision - using a deterministic rule engine, not an LLM.

PROTOTYPE 02 LIVE
Agent Governance Console
A runtime governance dashboard for autonomous AI agents. Demonstrates permission scoping (allow / block / approval-required), immutable action audit logs with decision traces, one-click kill switches, and incident management - mapped to the Singapore Agentic AI Framework (Jan 2026) and NIST AI RMF Manage function.

Bias & Fairness Audit Tool
A pre-deployment fairness analysis tool. Upload model prediction data and demographic attributes to compute industry-standard fairness metrics: disparate impact ratio, demographic parity difference, and equal opportunity difference - with plain-language interpretations and remediation guidance mapped to NIST AI RMF Measure.
